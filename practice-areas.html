Practice Areas

Eight areas where research-grounded expertise creates real value

We concentrate our practice in eight areas rather than a generalized caseload — spanning international trade, regulatory, environmental, and commercial matters.

💬 WhatsApp ✉ Email
Flagship Practice Area

I. International Trade & WTO Law

We advise on WTO dispute analysis, GATT 1994, the TBT and SPS Agreements, trade remedies (anti-dumping, countervailing, and safeguard measures), market-access regulation, non-tariff measures, and import/export restrictions. Our work includes WTO-consistency assessments, dispute strategy and legal research, analysis of WTO jurisprudence, and support for international trade negotiations.

Particular specialization: Trade and Environmental Regulation, including Article XX GATT, environmental exceptions, PPM measures, climate-related trade measures, carbon border measures, deforestation regulation, biodiversity-related trade restrictions, and unilateral environmental measures. This is our most distinctive area of practice.

II. International Economic Law

Research and advisory work on international economic agreements, treaty interpretation, bilateral investment treaties, investment protection, investor–state dispute analysis, and how state regulatory measures affect investment. We assist Indonesian companies expanding overseas, foreign investors in Indonesia, government-related institutions, research institutions, international organizations, universities, and law firms requiring specialist research support.

III. Trade, Environment & Sustainability

Where our academic specialization becomes commercially distinctive. We advise on environmental trade measures, climate-related trade regulation, Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM) analysis, deforestation-related trade regulation, EU environmental regulation, Multilateral Environmental Agreements, CITES-related matters, biodiversity and trade, sustainable supply-chain regulation, and extraterritorial environmental measures — including emerging frameworks such as the EU CBAM, the EU Deforestation Regulation, CITES, the Paris Agreement, the Convention on Biological Diversity, and the UNFCCC.

IV. Customs, Export–Import & Trade Remedies

Customs law, import/export regulation, customs valuation, tariff classification, customs disputes, import and export licensing, anti-dumping duties, safeguard measures, countervailing duties, and trade-remedy investigations, including matters before KADI and the Ministry of Trade. We also conduct Trade Compliance Reviews — assessing whether a company's supply chain and import/export practices comply with Indonesian trade and customs regulations.

V. Regulatory & Administrative Law

Regulatory compliance, government licensing, administrative decisions, regulatory interpretation, permits, and regulatory risk assessment. Our particular strength is complex regulatory interpretation — for example, how a new regulation interacts with existing rules, and what that means for a client's legal position.

VI. Complex Commercial Disputes

Selective, research-intensive dispute work — breach of contract, commercial claims, default (wanprestasi), unlawful acts (perbuatan melawan hukum), commercial settlement, legal opinions for disputes, and litigation strategy, including regulatory disputes that affect commercial relationships.

VII. Competition Law

Competition-law compliance, anti-competitive conduct, abuse of dominant position, cartel analysis, merger and acquisition competition issues, and matters before KPPU — with particular attention to the intersection of competition, trade, and regulation.

VIII. Tax & Economic Regulation

Specialized advisory on tax-law research, tax regulatory analysis, the interaction between customs and tax, import taxation, trade-related taxation, and cross-border tax and regulatory research.
