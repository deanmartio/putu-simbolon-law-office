Publications

Twenty-six published works on trade, investment, and environmental law

Matthew Simbolon's research has been published in more than two dozen academic outputs — including Scopus-indexed journals — on subjects spanning WTO law, international investment law, international environmental law, and comparative law. He also serves as a peer reviewer and editorial contributor for several academic journals.
