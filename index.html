Our Approach

Rigorous analysis for problems procedure alone can't solve

Complex legal problems require more than procedural advice — they require rigorous analysis. We take a research-oriented approach to legal practice, particularly for matters involving multiple legal regimes, regulatory interaction, international obligations, or unsettled questions of law.

Our work draws on Indonesian civil and commercial law, international economic law, WTO law, international investment law, international environmental law, and comparative legal research — with particular attention to where domestic regulation intersects with international economic relations and environmental objectives.
